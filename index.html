Photo Assignment (Spec || Editing || Slotting)

To request photos for a story:

  1. Open the "hamburger" menu (top left) and choose Assignments.
  2. Click + Create assignment.
  3. General info
    • Set Assignment type to Media.
    • Fill in a descriptive title and detailed description. This is your only spot to include notes for the photographer, such as specific requests or framing. Requests for video go here too.
    • For Slug, put your full name (so we know who asked for photo). This is how we know who asked.
    Click Next.
  4. Workflow and user info
    • Click the magnifying glass beside Assigned users.
    • Search by name and pick Sean-Allen2. That's not an example: Pick Sean for all of them, and make sure it's his @torstar.ca account.
    • Due date: Required. This tells the photographer whether to file right away or whether it can wait.
    • Number of assets (how many photos you want) is optional, but helpful.
    Click Next.
  5. Publication date: Required. Make your best guess at when the photo will run, then pick a date a few days after that. We search by this date, so don't leave it on the default. The rest of this screen can be skipped. Click Next.
  6. Venue: Address, city, and start and end time of the event. This is essential for the photographer. Click Next.
  7. Contact: Name, email and cell for the person the photographer is meeting. Click Next.
  8. Sections (this should be familiar from story assets)
    • Click Set sections.
    • Click the + beside publications to open the list. Easy to miss.
    • Check hamilton_spectator, then Add sections.
    • Confirm publications/hamilton_spectator is listed, then click Create assignment.

What happens next: Cloud Robot

A Slack bot, Cloud Robot, watches every assignment filed to Sean-Allen2.

  • Within about five minutes of you clicking Create assignment, your request posts in #spec-photo as Needs a photographer.
  • When a photographer is assigned, the full job goes to their own Slack channel, and the #spec-photo post flips to Assigned.
  • If you change the assignment in BLOX (time, place, notes), the photographer gets a short notice showing only what changed.
  • Every job has a number, like #14, on all its posts.
  • Ask it questions in any channel it's in: @Cloud Robot what's Cathie got tomorrow? or @Cloud Robot anything unassigned this week? or @Cloud Robot what's running Friday? (that one uses the publication date) Replies take 10 to 30 seconds.
  • Fix a job by its number: @Cloud Robot #14 when: Fri 2-3 p.m. also works for where:, note:, due: and photog:. Change it in BLOX too. BLOX is the record.
  • Every evening at 7:55 p.m., it posts a look-ahead of the next four days, with the weekend counted as one, in #spec-photo, and each photographer gets their own in their channel. Double-bookings are flagged.
  • It only knows what's in BLOX. It never contacts sources, and its answers leave out contact details.
